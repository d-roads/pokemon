The numbers, explained.

Market price & buy target

Prices use the narrowest 30-, 90-, or 180-day window with at least 3 matching sales. Smaller samples are shown with a limited-sample label. A buy target also requires a sale in the last 90 days. By default, the target is 15% below that median. You can choose your own target on a watched card.

PSA 9 and PSA 10 sales are separate. Raw targets use only listings explicitly identified as near mint. Mixed-condition raw guides and mixed-grader Grade 9 guides are shown as reference estimates, and are never used on their own to calculate a buy target.

Demand estimate / 100

This is a sales-activity proxy for this grade, not a measured popularity or investment rating. It combines comparable sales in the last 90 days (7 points per sale, capped at 70) and recency (up to 30 points). Raw demand uses near-mint sales. Sparse coverage can understate demand. “—” means no observations.

Sales & confidence

PriceCharting reports completed sales from eBay and TCGPlayer. Eyevo raw guides are reference estimates and do not provide the matching transactions used for targets. These transactions have not been independently verified. Best-offer prices may differ from original asking prices. Bundles, other graders, wrong card numbers, and ambiguous conditions are excluded from matching sales. Prices below 40% or above 250% of the matching median are excluded from targets. Small samples receive limited confidence.

Five-year scenarios

Bear, steady, and strong scenarios apply illustrative annual changes of −7%, +5%, and +12% to the current comparable median. They are assumptions, not forecasts or backtested predictions. They exclude selling fees, tax, and inflation.

Freshness

Price snapshots show their original observation date. The new sets include raw reference estimates from Eyevo where available; graded coverage varies. Refresh attempts request the latest publicly available source data. If the source is unavailable or its format changes, saved observations keep their original date. A successful fetch does not make an old transaction recent.
