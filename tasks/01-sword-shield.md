# Task 1 — Sword & Shield (start now)

Scope: all released English Sword & Shield cards and subsets from 2019–2023, including SWSH Black Star Promos, Shining Fates Shiny Vault, Trainer Galleries, Crown Zenith Galarian Gallery, Pokémon Futsal Collection, McDonald's 2021/2022, Celebrations and its Classic Collection, and Pokémon GO.

The catalog is generated by `node tools/research/gen-modern.mjs swsh`. Capture all exact-matched rare/promo source pages with `node tools/research/capture-modern.mjs swsh`; the command resumes per card and records unmatched products and errors in `tools/research/modern-coverage-swsh.json`. Count only card/printing/grade/date-valid sale rows exposed by each public page. Earlier or unreported eBay sales are outside the source snapshot; never label it complete eBay history. Leave unmatched cards unpriced, without scores or trend projections. Run the evidence audit, full tests, and Worker build. Verify the expanded information view on both Sword & Shield and WOTC cards. Preserve the local user SQLite database.

Do not merge this task with Task 2; record results and limitations separately.

Result on October 6, 2026: 28 sets, 3,712 cards, 2,290 eligible rare/promo cards. The final offline audit covers 2,285 captured exact product pages and 134,238 accepted sale rows, 70,913 labeled eBay, with zero invalid accepted rows. Five cards lack a saved exact product capture; the coverage report names the unresolved matches and one page-name error. PSA 10 and PSA 9 projections passing the historical holdout total 98 and 47 respectively; future returns remain unverified. The extended information view is shared by these cards and the WOTC catalog. All 119 Node tests and the Worker build pass.
